# International report example

**Purpose:** Approved style and substance reference for International reporter output and editor rollups.

**Date of example:** 2026-07-26

**Important:** This is a dated example, not a source of current facts. Future runs must not copy its titles, facts, dates, figures, wording, or links. Recreate the reporting from the current coverage window and current sources.

## What this demonstrates

- Give more weight to developments that recur across independent sources, while still checking the quality and role of each source.
- Make the expanded body copy informative: state what happened, explain why it matters, add context, and say what happens next or remains uncertain.
- Keep verified facts, attributed claims, analysis, conflicts, and uncertainty visibly separate.
- Use measured desk voice without flattening consequences or avoiding difficult facts.
- Use the bibliography to show the evidence; do not spend the body copy defending why the editor selected the card.
- A disagreement in figures or accounts is useful information when it is named and explained rather than silently reconciled.

## Example card 1

### The Iran war is widening even as the US pauses its strikes

**Subtitle:** Houthi attacks near Saudi oil infrastructure and Iran's accusation against Ukraine extend the conflict to Red Sea and Caspian shipping.

**Status:** Developing  
**Confidence:** High for the reported incidents; medium for the scale of physical damage.  
**Why it belongs:** AP, Al Jazeera, and Reuters-linked reporting describe related developments across two strategic waterways, making this more than a single-front military update.

The United States appeared to pause its air campaign against Iran after 13 nights of strikes, with Oman again involved in contacts aimed at keeping a wider war from becoming permanent. The pause did not stop the conflict from spreading geographically. Houthi forces said they launched missiles and drones toward Saudi Arabia's Jizan and Yanbu areas after Saudi-led strikes on Hodeidah. Saudi and coalition accounts disputed parts of that description, so the existence of the exchange is better established than the full damage assessment.

The geography matters. The Bab el-Mandeb and the Strait of Hormuz sit on different ends of the region's shipping system, but disruption at either point can raise freight, insurance, and energy costs well beyond the immediate battlefield. A second thread involving Iran's accusation that Ukraine helped attack Caspian shipping is less settled, but it shows how quickly the conflict's diplomatic and commercial consequences could extend outside the Gulf. Those claims should remain attributed until independent evidence clarifies them.

The next signal is whether the US pause becomes a negotiating channel or only an operational interval. Markets, shipping companies, and neighboring governments will be watching for further attacks, formal statements from Tehran and Washington, and evidence that the Saudi incidents caused sustained infrastructure or supply disruption. The immediate facts are cross-source supported; the broader regional-war interpretation remains an assessment, not a settled outcome.

## Example card 2

### Congo's Ebola outbreak is accelerating while treatment workers strike

**Subtitle:** The outbreak has surpassed 1,300 reported deaths, while conflicting updates and a labor dispute show the response losing ground.

**Status:** Ongoing  
**Confidence:** High for the outbreak and response breakdown; medium for the latest totals because the figures differ by update.  
**Why it belongs:** AP and Al Jazeera both report a rapidly worsening outbreak, while WHO material supplies the public-health context and formal response status.

Health workers at an Ebola treatment center in Bunia went on strike over unpaid bonuses as the disease continued to spread in the Democratic Republic of Congo. An AP update cited 2,973 cases and 1,309 deaths. A later Al Jazeera report gave a higher count of 3,075 cases and more than 1,354 deaths. The difference may reflect reporting dates, delayed case confirmation, or a revised surveillance series; it should not be presented as a contradiction without checking the underlying bulletin.

The practical significance is that the response system is being weakened at the point where speed matters most. More than 100 health workers have reportedly been infected, while conflict, population movement, attacks on health facilities, and mistrust make contact tracing and isolation harder. WHO has warned that the emergency could expand across borders, and the available response does not remove the need for rapid diagnosis, safe care, and sustained local staffing.

The next questions are whether the strike is resolved, whether new treatment capacity reaches affected areas, and whether official surveillance can produce a consistent daily picture. The mortality figures in this example are therefore marked as time-sensitive. The underlying story is stronger than any single total: a serious outbreak is advancing while the people needed to contain it report that the response is failing them.

## Example card 3

### Deadly West Bank clashes trigger a major Israeli military clampdown

**Subtitle:** Four Palestinians and two Israeli soldiers were killed near Nablus as mass detentions and raids followed.

**Status:** Developing  
**Confidence:** Medium-high; the deaths and subsequent operation are widely reported, but accounts of responsibility and some operational details remain contested.  
**Why it belongs:** AP, Al Jazeera, Reuters-linked coverage, The Washington Post, and Le Monde all place the incident within a wider escalation involving settlers, Palestinian communities, and Israeli forces.

Israeli forces launched a large operation in the northern West Bank after clashes near Nablus in which four Palestinians and two Israeli soldiers were reported killed. The Israeli military said more than 70 people were detained. Accounts differ over how the fighting began and who was responsible for particular deaths, so those claims require attribution rather than being collapsed into a single narrative. Reports of raids and pressure on local medical facilities add a civilian-access dimension to the security operation.

The incident matters because it combines a deadly confrontation with a rapid expansion of arrests and military presence. That pattern can restrict movement, delay medical care, and increase the risk of retaliatory violence. It also places pressure on the Palestinian Authority, Israeli government, and outside governments that are already struggling to contain settlement-related violence and prevent a wider regional crisis from producing another front.

The next evidence will be the formal charges, independent accounts of the deaths, and whether the operation remains concentrated around Nablus or broadens to other areas. The central facts are sufficiently corroborated for a developing card, but the causal account is not settled. Any summary that names responsibility should identify whose account it is and what has not been independently established.

## Example card 4

### India's education minister resigns after weeks of youth protests

**Subtitle:** Dharmendra Pradhan's resignation is the first major concession from the Modi government after demonstrations over exam leaks grew into an accountability movement.

**Status:** Verified  
**Confidence:** High.  
**Why it belongs:** AP, Al Jazeera, Reuters-linked reporting, The Guardian, and Euronews-linked coverage connect the resignation to the same sustained protest movement and examination controversy.

Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan resigned after weeks of protests over alleged examination leaks and irregularities. The demonstrations, described in reporting as the Cockroach movement, began with demands for accountability around the exams but widened into a challenge to the government's handling of education and public institutions. The resignation is confirmed; the underlying allegations remain allegations until the relevant investigations establish which exams were compromised and how.

The political significance is larger than a cabinet change. Students and young graduates have made access to trustworthy examinations a test of whether effort can still produce a fair opportunity, while the government must now show that a personnel change will be followed by credible reforms. Reports of police using tear gas and batons during demonstrations add to the movement's sense that the issue is about treatment as well as administration.

The next test will be whether the government publishes a transparent account of the disputed examinations, provides remedies to affected students, and changes the system that allowed the irregularities to occur. A resignation can acknowledge political pressure without resolving the practical problem. The report should therefore distinguish the verified political event from the still-developing factual record behind the exam claims.

## Example card 5

### Flávio Bolsonaro secures Brazil's presidential nomination amid US tensions

**Subtitle:** Jair Bolsonaro's son will challenge Lula without a running mate, as the campaign begins under a worsening dispute with Washington.

**Status:** Verified  
**Confidence:** High for the nomination and current diplomatic measures; medium for the eventual coalition shape.  
**Why it belongs:** AP, Reuters-linked reporting, Al Jazeera, and El País describe the nomination, its international endorsements, and the unresolved domestic and US-Brazilian political consequences.

Brazil's Liberal Party confirmed Flávio Bolsonaro as its presidential candidate. The launch included support from Argentina's Javier Milei, a message from his father Jair Bolsonaro, and a video from Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. Flávio began without a running mate or an established centrist coalition, leaving the candidate with a recognizable family name but an incomplete electoral structure as he prepares to challenge President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.

The campaign is also starting inside a worsening dispute with the United States. Washington has imposed tariffs of up to 37.5% on Brazilian goods, and Brazil has denied visas to two US State Department officials. The two developments are related to the political environment but should not be treated as proof that the nomination will produce a particular foreign-policy outcome. Tariffs, visa measures, and campaign positioning are facts; predictions about their effect on the election are analysis.

The next questions are whether Flávio can attract a running mate and broader conservative alliance, how his father's legal and political position affects the campaign, and whether the dispute with Washington changes trade or voter priorities before the October election. The nomination is settled; the durability of the coalition and the electoral consequences remain open.

## Editor takeaway

The best card is not necessarily the one with the most dramatic title. It is the one where repeated reporting identifies a meaningful development, the body gives the reader usable facts and consequences, and the remaining uncertainty is named precisely. Preserve this balance when adapting the pattern to other desks and to the larger News and Sports rollups.
